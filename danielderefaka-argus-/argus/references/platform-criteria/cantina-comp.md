## Severity definitions — Impact × Likelihood matrix

|  | Impact: High | Impact: Medium | Impact: Low |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Likelihood: High** | High | High | Medium |
| **Likelihood: Medium** | High | Medium | Low |
| **Likelihood: Low** | Medium | Low | Informational |

### High (Impact: High)
- Funds can be directly lost

### Medium
- Impact High with Likelihood Low
- Impact Medium with Likelihood Medium or High

### Low
- Impact Low with Likelihood Medium or High
- Impact Medium with Likelihood Low

### Informational
- Impact Low with Likelihood Low
- Design issues without fund loss

**Tiebreak guide**: ask what happens to the protocol if the bug is NOT fixed. Catastrophic + triggerable by anyone → very likely High. Protocol can function without fix → likely Low.

## Automatic invalidators

| ID | Rule | Result |
|----|------|--------|
| AI-1 | User error manageable from frontend | Informational at most |
| AI-2 | Requires admin / authority access (unless protocol designed to be resilient) | Low at most |
| AI-3 | **AI-generated finding without manual validation** | INVALID + risk of ban |
| AI-4 | Approval / ERC-20 / SPL race condition | INVALID |
| AI-5 | Non-standard / weird tokens (unless mentioned in README) | Low at most |
| AI-6 | Dust amounts / rounding errors | Low at most |
| AI-7 | Finding acknowledged in previous report | INVALID |
| AI-8 | High or Medium without PoC (researcher reputation < 80) | INVALID — PoC mandatory |
| AI-9 | PoC that doesn't compile or doesn't demonstrate impact | INVALID |
| AI-10 | Suggested fix goes against the protocol's design philosophy | Informational at most |

## ⚠️ Critical: AI-generated content policy

Cantina **actively enforces AI-generated finding detection.** Submitting AI-generated findings without manual validation is grounds for INVALID + ban.

**Argus is AI tooling.** Findings produced by Argus pipelines are AI-generated by definition. Before submitting any Argus finding to a Cantina competition or bounty, the user MUST:
1. Manually re-read the cited code at the cited line numbers.
2. Independently re-derive the exploit path from first principles.
3. Run the PoC themselves and confirm the output matches the verdict.md claim.
4. Re-verify the impact against the program's specific scope (not Argus's general analysis).
5. Re-write the writeup in their own words — do not paste Argus output verbatim.

Stage 5 will refuse to ADVANCE any finding to Stage 6 with a Cantina platform target unless the run includes a manual-validation acknowledgment from the user. See `platform-validation.md` for the gate.

## PoC requirements

By default, ALL Cantina competitions have **mandatory PoC**:
- High and Medium require coded PoC before competition ends
- Applies to researchers with reputation < 80
- Exceptions: missing functions, or researcher with reputation ≥ 80
- PoC MUST compile and demonstrate impact
- Cantina Dedicated Researchers can provide PoC on request

PoC that doesn't compile or doesn't demonstrate impact → INVALID (AI-9).

## Quality signals

| Signal | Full credit | Partial / downgrade | Invalidation risk |
|--------|------------|---------------------|-------------------|
| Root cause | Clearly identified in in-scope code | Symptom only | High |
| Impact × Likelihood justification | Both dimensions argued with evidence | Only impact stated | High — judge recalculates severity |
| PoC | Compiles, executes, demonstrates impact | Doesn't fully prove | Critical — AI-8/9 invalidates |
| Attack path | Step-by-step with realistic conditions | Partial | Moderate |
| Remediation | Aligned with protocol design | Goes against design | Moderate — AI-10 caps Informational |
| Severity alignment | Matches matrix | Misaligned | High — recalculation |

Key emphasis:
- MUST justify both Impact AND Likelihood explicitly.
- PoC is not optional for High/Medium under reputation 80.
- Fix goes against design → entire finding capped Informational.

## Scope

- In-scope → parents included
- Library used by in-scope → valid
- Out-of-scope → INVALID

## Duplicate rules

- Same root cause = duplicates
- Fixing root cause makes both unexploitable

## Escalation

After judging there is an escalation phase. Penalty for invalid escalations. Weak evidence + escalation penalty can be worse than accepting original verdict.
